Quick-Review Sheet: The Numbers That Trip You Up

TINEA

Corporis: topical azole BID, include margin of normal skin, 1–4 wk
• NO antifungal+steroid combos
• Griseofulvin = systemic drug of choice >2 yr, 2–4 wk (non-scalp), take with FATTY food
• Extended griseofulvin: CBC + LFT + renal at 8 wk, then every 8 wk
• Back to school 24 h after treatment starts
• F/u 2 wk; no response → culture + switch class
• KOH from lesion BORDER
• Tinea incognito = steroid/calcineurin cream → improves then rebounds

Cruris: topical 4–6 wk
• spares penis, scrotum, labia majora (Candida does NOT spare scrotum + has satellites)
• socks BEFORE underwear

Pedis: apply 1 cm beyond border BID until 7 days AFTER clearing; usual 3–6 wk
• griseofulvin (rare) 6–8 wk
• 3rd–5th toe webs; dorsum clear
• F/u 2–3 wk
• sports OK
• foul odor = secondary bacterial

Versicolor (Malassezia): selenium sulfide 2.5% thin layer 30 min, 2×/wk × 2–4 wk → monthly × 3 mo
• topical azole BID 2–4 wk
• oral: fluconazole 400 mg ONCE or itraconazole 400 mg ONCE → exercise/sweat after
• F/u 1 mo
• no flaking on scrape = working
• repigmentation takes MONTHS

SEBORRHEA & DIAPER

Seb derm: NOT pruritic, no pustules
• infant: mineral oil 5–10 min → mild shampoo, soft brush; NO medicated shampoo <2 yr
• teen face: ketoconazole 2% daily, desonide 0.05% intermittent
• scalp shampoo 2–3×/wk (1–4×/mo African American), leave on 5–10 min
• F/u 1–2 wk
• severe generalized → think HIV

Diaper: change q1–2 h, ≥8/24 h
• HC 0.5–1% thin layer TID ≤5 DAYS, never fluorinated
• sitz 10–15 min QID
• irritant = convex, creases SPARED (peak 9–12 mo)
• Candida = folds + satellites
• Seborrhea = IN creases, greasy, onset 3–4 wk
• phone f/u 1–2 days, reassess within 1 wk

HERPES

HSV: incubation 2–12 d
• Tzanck = giant cells, CAN'T type
• viral culture = gold standard
• acyclovir 20–40 mg/kg/dose 5×/day × 5 d (>2 yr), best within 3 DAYS
• whitlow: 200 mg 5×/day × 5–10 d
• frequent recurrences: prophylaxis × 6 mo
• diphenhydramine rinse max 5 mg/kg/d
• daycare exclusion ONLY gingivostomatitis + can't control secretions
• REFER: newborn, immunosuppressed, infected eczema, eye/lid

Zoster: does NOT cross midline
• acyclovir 30 mg/kg/d ÷ QID × 5 d only if immunosuppressed, ocular, Ramsay Hunt
• NO salicylates
• eye/forehead/NOSE → immediate ophtho
• new vesicles up to 1 wk, resolve 2–3 wk
• covered lesions = can attend school
• >1 dermatome → think HIV

MOLLUSCUM & WARTS

Molluscum: 1–6 mm umbilicated, spares palms/soles/scalp
• resolves 6 mo–2 yr (up to 4)
• NO school exclusion
• molluscum dermatitis → moisturizer, AVOID hydrocortisone
• genital → abuse eval
• hundreds → HIV

Warts: salicylic >20% × 4–6 wk (not >5 mm)
• 40% plaster 3–5 d = plantar
• retinoic acid 0.025–0.05% = FLAT warts only
• duct tape 12 h/day × 6 days
• cryo 2–10 s, 1–3 mm beyond
• cantharidin 0.7% × 24 h = periungual/plantar ONLY
• podophyllum wash off at 4 h
• cimetidine 20–30 mg/kg/d BID × 3–4 mo
• imiquimod >12 yr daily × 1–2 mo
• filiform → scissors
• F/u 2–3 wk

CONTACT DERMATITIS

Irritant = immediate; allergic = type IV DELAYED
• compresses 20 min q4–6 h
• topical steroid 2–3×/day: relief 2–3 d, healing 2–3 wk
• ORAL steroid if >10% BSA: 10–14 d, taper last 7
• no lanolin emollient if inflamed
• worsens/recurs → patch testing

EXANTHEMS

Roseola (HHV-6): 7–24 mo
• high fever 3–7 d, child looks well → rash as fever BREAKS, trunk outward
• febrile seizure 10–15%
• most contagious during fever

Measles: incubation 8–12 d
• 3 C's + Koplik (opposite lower molars)
• rash day 3–4, hairline → down
• contagious ~4 d before to 4 d after rash
• AIRBORNE
• report on suspicion (≤24 h)
• Vitamin A × 2 days: <6 mo 50,000 IU
• 6–11 mo 100,000
• ≥12 mo 200,000
• MMR within 72 H
• immune globulin within 6 DAYS
• pneumonia = top killer
• SSPE late, fatal

Mumps: incubation 16–18 d (12–25)
• contagious 1–2 d before to 5 d after swelling
• exclusion: your text says 9 days; current CDC/AAP says 5 days
• ear pushed up and out, "pickle sign"
• ↑ amylase
• orchitis ~10% postpubertal males, sterility rare

Rubella: incubation 14–21 d (mean 18)
• shedding 5 d before to 6 d after rash
• postauricular / posterior cervical / occipital nodes
• Forchheimer spots NOT pathognomonic
• IgM may be negative before day 5 of rash → repeat
• 1st 12 wk: 61% defects; 2nd trimester: 26%
• home ~7 days after rash
• arthritis in postpubertal females

Fifth (parvovirus B19): slapped cheek → lacy rash on body
• NOT contagious once rash appears (no exclusion)
• aplastic crisis in sickle cell/hemolytic anemia
• hydrops fetalis risk in pregnancy
• adults: symmetric hand/wrist/knee arthritis
• supportive care
