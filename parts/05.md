D. "Your child must stay out of childcare until the rash has completely faded, because the rash is the most contagious stage."
E. "Apply a topical corticosteroid to the rash twice daily until it clears."
F. "An antiviral such as acyclovir will shorten the fever if started today."

**Answer:** A, B, C
**Rationale:** The roseola rash is nonpruritic and lasts hours to 2 to 3 days, so it needs no specific treatment. Antipyretics are reasonable for comfort. **Q232. [Single]** Which statement best describes the relationship between fever and rash that helps distinguish measles from roseola?
A. In both measles and roseola, the temperature rises as the rash appears.

**Q236. [Single]** A healthy, unvaccinated 2-year-old received IGIM (0.5 mL/kg) 5 days after a measles exposure and never developed measles. When should she receive MMR vaccine?

B. 4 weeks after the IGIM dose
C. 6 months after the IGIM dose

**Answer:** C
**Rationale:** Passively acquired measles antibody interferes with the response to live MMR vaccine. **Q241. [SATA]** Which statements about the measles virus and its epidemiology are accurate? Select all that apply.
A. Measles virus has only one antigenic type.
B. Humans and primates are the only known reservoirs of infection.
C. Peak incidence in susceptible people occurs in late summer and fall.
D. Infectious virus is present in respiratory secretions, blood, and urine.
E. A chronic carrier state develops in a minority of recovered patients.
F. A single dose of measles vaccine reliably gives lifelong immunity.

**Answer:** A, B, D
**Rationale:** Measles is a Morbillivirus in the Paramyxoviridae family with a single antigenic type.
