**Rationale:** A mild illness with postauricular and suboccipital nodes and discrete maculopapules that start on the face, spread downward within 24 hours, and fade quickly is classic rubella. Scarlet fever has a sandpaper-like rash with circumoral pallor and pharyngitis.

**Q300. [Single]** Four 6-year-olds in the same classroom were exposed to a classmate who developed fifth disease. For which child is the exposure most concerning for transient aplastic crisis?
A. A child with sickle cell disease (HbSS) and a baseline reticulocyte count of 12%
B. A child whose iron deficiency anemia resolved with 3 months of oral iron
C. A child who is a silent carrier of alpha-thalassemia with normal red cell indices
D. A child with sickle cell trait (HbAS) and a normal baseline hemoglobin

**Answer:** A
**Rationale:** Transient aplastic crisis happens when B19 halts red cell production in a patient whose red cells already have a shortened lifespan, as in sickle cell disease or hereditary spherocytosis. That is why a high baseline reticulocyte count signals vulnerability.
